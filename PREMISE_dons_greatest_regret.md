# The Don's Greatest Regret — premise for the 60-clip run

Paste everything below the line into the dashboard's premise box. It is deliberately dense: this text is
injected into all 60 clip prompts, so every padded sentence costs ~60x.

What changed from your draft, and why:

- **Appearances added.** Four characters, four different hair colours (black / auburn / silver / brown), each
  with a distinguishing feature. The validator rejects two characters sharing a hair colour in a cast this
  size, and your draft gave none — left to the model, Elena and Camilla would both have come out dark-haired
  women in dark coats, which is how the last run lost track of who was who.
- **Voices added.** A character's first spoken line is cut out and reused as their voice for the rest of the
  film, and on that first clip the written description is the only thing shaping it. No description meant a
  flat invented narrator reused for all five minutes.
- **Marco is secretly Elena's.** Your Act 3 had Elena absent, which breaks the first-person machinery: a
  voiceover clip with people on screen must include the protagonist, and so must a wordless reaction beat.
  Marco keeping an open line to her puts her in the scene, reuses the phone device from Act 2, and lands the
  reveal on her and the audience at the same moment instead of second-hand.
- **Reveal order stated**, so Act 1 can ache about the cost without naming what Act 3 exists to reveal.

**THE DON'S GREATEST REGRET — a 5-act crime drama, 60 clips, 12 per act.**

**CASTING NOTE — nobody in this cast is old.** They are all between their late twenties and late thirties:
young, sharp, expensive-looking people. Lorenzo went silver early and it reads as money, not age. No lines
on faces, no stooping, no frailty — this is a world of people at the height of their power.

**POV PROTAGONIST (all narration is her first-person voice): Elena Vance.** A woman of about thirty, chief
strategist who ran the Moretti syndicate's logistics for three years. Jet black hair in a severe low chignon;
a thin pale scar along her jaw; tall and spare; charcoal tailored coat over a high-necked black dress. Voice:
a low, controlled alto with banked fury under total calm — she never raises it.

**OTHER CHARACTERS**
- **Lorenzo Moretti**, the Don. A man in his late thirties, hard and lean, silver-grey hair gone early and
  worn short, clean-shaven, broad through the shoulders; black three-piece suit, gold signet ring. Voice: a
  gravelled baritone used to being obeyed.
- **Camilla Rossi**, rival cartel heiress. A woman in her late twenties, auburn waves worn loose, heavy gold
  drop earrings, slight and quick; wine-red silk. Voice: a bright, lacquered soprano that smiles through
  everything.
- **Marco**, Lorenzo's capo — and secretly Elena's man. A man in his early thirties, dark brown hair cropped
  close, a badly set broken nose, square and still; grey overcoat. Voice: a quiet, flat bass that never
  hurries.

**SETTING** — five locations, one per act: `moretti_penthouse_office` (mahogany desk, floor-to-ceiling storm
windows, crystal decanter); `vance_harbor_terminal` (glass control room above the container yard, radio
console, wall of shipping screens); `moretti_private_vault` (reinforced steel room, deposit walls, a bank of
blinking surveillance monitors); `harbor_pier_storm` (rain-lashed industrial pier, security gates, amber
floodlights on black puddles); `freighter_gangway_stairs` (lit steel boarding stairs, vast black hull,
churning sea).

**ACT 1 — THE COLD BETRAYAL (clips 1-12), penthouse office.** Three years Elena kept Lorenzo out of a federal
cell and ahead of every ambush. Tonight, to buy the Rossi alliance, he slides a termination contract across
the desk while Camilla watches from the shadows. He tells her to sign and walk out with nothing. Elena does
not argue and does not weep. She works her heavy platinum ring off her finger, drops it onto the signature
line, tells him he has just thrown away the only thing standing between him and the dark, and walks out into
the rain.

**ACT 2 — HIS EMPIRE CRUMBLES (clips 13-24), Vance harbour terminal.** Elena takes command of her family's
maritime fleet. Inside forty-eight hours Lorenzo's world seizes: lanes close, accounts freeze, his trucks are
taken on the road. Camilla is useless. He calls Elena's private line to demand she open the ports. She lets
him finish, tells him the loyalty he thought he owned was always hers, and hangs up.

**ACT 3 — THE VAULT (clips 25-36), Moretti private vault.** Lorenzo and Marco catch Camilla draining the
vault and lifting the encrypted ledgers. Marco draws. **Marco has an open line to Elena throughout — she
hears all of it, off screen, and it is her narration that carries the act.** Cornered, Camilla laughs in
Lorenzo's face and tells him what he never knew: Elena signed the false customs manifests herself and took
the criminal blame to keep him out of prison, while Camilla fed his movements to the men who ambushed him.
Lorenzo banishes her on pain of death and sends every car to the harbour.

**ACT 4 — THE CHASE IN THE RAIN (clips 37-48), harbour pier.** The convoy slews to a stop at the gates.
Lorenzo gets out into the downpour, soaked, past caring who sees, and shouts down the pier while Elena
finishes her clearance without turning round. He asks her price. She tells him some debts cannot be paid with
dirty money.

**ACT 5 — TOO LATE TO BEG (clips 49-60), freighter gangway.** Elena turns on the boarding stairs and looks
down at him. The Don who knelt to no one goes down into the mud, hands out, begging her not to go — he cannot
rule without her. She looks at him with nothing at all in her face and tells him he ruled by fear, and fear
cannot buy back the woman who gave him everything. The foghorn goes, the gates slam, she steps into the lit
cabin, and the ship pulls out into the storm with him still on his knees.

**REVEAL ORDER.** Act 1 may ache about what loyalty cost her, but no line before Act 3 — hers or anyone's —
may name the false customs manifests, the criminal blame she took, or Camilla's treachery. Those three are
Act 3's to disclose. "Vance Maritime" and the ports belong to Act 2. The encrypted ledgers belong to Act 3.

**JOLTS** (wordless beats, only where the story really has one): the platinum ring dropping onto the contract
and spinning flat; the terminal floodlights banging on across the container yard; the receiver going down;
Marco's weapon coming up; the ledger case tipping and spilling; the convoy slewing to a halt in standing
water; the gangway foghorn and the security gates slamming. **In Act 3 the wordless beats are the room
itself — the vault door, the monitor wall, the spilled case — with nobody in frame**, because Elena is only
ever a voice on Marco's line there.

**TONE.** Rain-lit crime drama. Quiet, controlled, nothing shouted except Lorenzo at the gates. Elena's
narration is close and unhurried — a woman telling you exactly what it cost, while it is still costing.

**NARRATION WEIGHT.** This is a narration-led story: lean to the upper end of the voiceover range, two
voiceover beats per cycle wherever the story allows, so Elena's inner voice carries the film. Her narration
is never a caption for what is on screen — it says the thing the picture cannot.

**OPENING.** Clip 1 is Elena's voice over her own stillness before anyone speaks — something to the effect of
*"Three years I kept that man breathing. Tonight he hands me a pen."* Then straight into Lorenzo sliding the
contract across the desk. Make the lines sharper than mine.
